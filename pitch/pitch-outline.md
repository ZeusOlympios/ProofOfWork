# Pitch Outline — ProofOfWork

## Slide 1 — The idea
ProofOfWork: Verified skills. Visible potential.
Challenge: Graduate Employability.
One sentence: Graduates submit a real project, complete a short practical task, and receive evidence-linked scores against a transparent rubric so employers can assess capability beyond work history.

## Slide 2 — The problem
Graduates can have skills without formal work history. Employers have limited time to verify project quality. Experience becomes a proxy for ability.

## Slide 3 — The system
Stakeholders: graduates, employers, reviewers, universities and training providers. Each has different incentives and constraints.

## Slide 4 — Root causes
Experience requirements, verification costs, unequal networks and inconsistent evidence reinforce the experience gap.

## Slide 5 — Intervention choice
Compare project-based scoring with employer micro-internships. Select project-based assessment for prototype feasibility, while acknowledging its verification limitations.

## Slide 6 — Proposed workflow
Submit repository -> reviewer assesses rubric and practical task -> evidence-linked scores -> anonymised employer profile -> shortlist.

## Slide 7 — Prototype
Show only the screens or artefacts that actually exist. If the application is not implemented, present a clearly labelled workflow diagram and documentation, not a fictitious live demo.

## Slide 8 — Evidence and validation
State what has actually been researched or tested. The initial plan proposes reviewer agreement, evidence traceability, usability and privacy checks. Do not invent results.

## Slide 9 — Feasibility
Proposed stack: React, ASP.NET Core Web API and PostgreSQL. Initial implementation would focus on one workflow and synthetic data.

## Slide 10 — Risks and trade-offs
Copied work, reviewer inconsistency, resource inequality, privacy, false precision and employer adoption.

## Slide 11 — Expected impact
Potentially better-informed shortlists and discovery of overlooked talent. Measure reviewer agreement, screening time, candidate completion and shortlist outcomes.

## Slide 12 — Next steps
Build the submission and review workflow, test the rubric with independent reviewers, then conduct a small consent-based pilot. Validate before making impact claims.
